Where to Paddle

Every lake, river and shoreline we deliver canoes to, with honest skill levels and local tips for each.

Home › Where to Paddle

New to paddling? Start with a Beginner lake like Cat Lake, Alice Lake or Lost Lake. Spots marked Intermediate have current, wind or open water, so build up to those. Not sure which is right? Call (604) 849-8898 and Jeremy will match a spot to your group.

Squamish

Alice LakeFour lakes, one park and a campground. We bring the canoe to your campsite or the beach so you skip the roof rack and the parking scramble.
Squamish · Beginner Brohm LakeA warm summer lake with cliffs, swim spots and a shoreline trail, 15 minutes north of Squamish. We deliver to the launch.
Squamish · Beginner Cat LakeSmall, calm and motor-free. Cat Lake is the easiest canoe day in Squamish, and the one we recommend to anyone who has never paddled.
Squamish · Beginner Browning LakeA small lake under granite cliffs in Murrin Provincial Park. It's compact, quiet and beautiful in evening light.
Squamish · Beginner Squamish RiverReal moving water through the valley under the Tantalus Range. We drop you upstream and pick you up downstream.
Squamish · Intermediate Squamish EstuaryTidal flatwater with the Stawamus Chief overhead. We help you time the tide so the launch is easy and the paddle is calm.
Squamish · Beginner-Intermediate Mamquam Blind ChannelA calm tidal channel right behind downtown Squamish. Grab a coffee, then paddle.
Squamish · Beginner Howe SoundSaltwater paddling in a fjord, with seals, eagles and Anvil Island on the horizon. Best in calm morning windows.
Squamish · Intermediate Echo LakePaddle across the Squamish River to the Echo Falls trailhead.
Squamish · Intermediate

Whistler

Alta LakeThe classic Whistler paddle, with three put-ins, warm water and mountain views. We deliver to Rainbow, Lakeside or Wayside Park.
Whistler · Beginner River of Golden DreamsWhistler's best half-day paddle. We drop you at Rainbow Park, you float down to Green Lake, and we pick you up at Meadow Park.
Whistler · Beginner-Intermediate Green LakeTurquoise glacial water under Wedge Mountain. Bigger, colder and more exposed than Whistler's other lakes, and that's the appeal.
Whistler · Intermediate Lost LakeWalking distance from Whistler Village, with no motorboats and plenty of swim breaks. The easy family pick.
Whistler · Beginner Nita LakeSmall, quiet and almost always glass-calm. Nita Lake is Whistler's sunrise and sunset paddle.
Whistler · Beginner Alpha LakeA calm, shallow Creekside lake with a beach park and dock. It's the easy pick with kids.
Whistler · Beginner

Pemberton

One Mile LakeWarmer, quieter water 25 minutes north of Whistler, with a family beach park and an easy launch.
Pemberton · Beginner
