Paddling Howe Sound

Howe Sound, delivered.

Howe Sound is North America's southernmost fjord: deep saltwater framed by granite walls and mountains rising straight from the shore. Paddling here is a different world from the lakes, with seals popping up beside the boat, eagles overhead and huge views in every direction.

We deliver to the Howe Sound launches along the Sea to Sky Highway, including Britannia Beach, Porteau Cove and Furry Creek, for half-day, full-day and overnight trips.

Local Knowledge

Tips for Howe Sound

Go early. Howe Sound's afternoon winds can build quickly and make the return hard work.

Stay close to shore and keep an eye on the weather.

Want to see more of the Sound without paddling? Our sister company Squamish Water Taxi runs boat trips to the islands.

Good to Know

Howe Sound Canoe Rental FAQ

Where do you deliver canoes on Howe Sound?

Britannia Beach, Porteau Cove and Furry Creek are our main Howe Sound launches. Ask about others.

Is Howe Sound safe for canoeing?

In calm conditions with some paddling experience, yes. Wind and waves can build in the afternoon, so go early and stay near shore.

Can I do an overnight Howe Sound trip?

Yes. Multi-day rentals are available, and the canoe stays with you for the trip.
