Good to Know

Squamish Boat Rental FAQ

Do I need a boat licence?

No. Every boat we rent is human-powered — canoes, paddleboards and rafts — so no Pleasure Craft Operator Card is required. Wear the PFD we provide and you're set.

What kinds of boats can I rent?

Canoes (up to 3 paddlers), stand-up paddleboards, and rafts. We don't rent motorboats. All gear is included; delivery is available and priced by location — call for a quote.

Where do you deliver?

Anywhere in Squamish — Alice Lake, Cat Lake, Brohm Lake, Browning Lake, the Squamish River, the Squamish Estuary, Mamquam Blind Channel, and Howe Sound launches like Britannia Beach and Porteau Cove. Campsites and Airbnbs too.

Can I get a motorboat or a boat ride instead?

Yes — through our sister service Squamish Water Taxi. If you hold a PCOC you can rent their 18-ft RIB and skipper Howe Sound yourself; otherwise book a guided wildlife tour or a sunrise or sunset cruise out of Porteau Cove and let the skipper drive.

Heading up to Whistler?

We deliver there too — see canoe delivery in Whistler and Whistler paddleboard delivery.
