Our Philosophy

We believe getting outside is the most important thing you can do for your health and happiness.

We're a locally owned and operated company serving the Sea to Sky Corridor — from Squamish to Whistler and Pemberton. Our fleet features personally refurbished canoes, paddleboards, and rafts, each maintained with care by owner Jeremy Allen.

Whether you're a first-timer looking to explore a calm lake or an experienced paddler seeking multi-day adventures on Howe Sound, we've got you covered.

What We Offer

Rentals & Equipment

Canoe Rentals

Hand-refurbished canoes for lake, river, and ocean adventures. Day-rated rentals include life jackets, paddles, bailers, and whistles.

Paddleboard Rentals

Stand-up paddleboards perfect for exploring the estuary, calm lakes, and coastal waters of Howe Sound.

Raft Rentals

Rafts available for group outings and calm water exploration. Perfect for families and larger parties.

Pickup & Dropoff

We deliver to your preferred launch spot across Squamish, Whistler, and Pemberton. Portable racks available for self-transport.
