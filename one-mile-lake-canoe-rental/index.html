Paddling One Mile Lake

One Mile Lake, delivered.

One Mile Lake sits at the edge of Pemberton, a small lake with a beach park, an easy launch and Mount Currie on the skyline. It's usually warmer and far less busy than the Whistler lakes in peak season.

It's a great escape when Whistler is packed, or an easy add-on if you're staying in Pemberton.

Local Knowledge

Tips for One Mile Lake

Go when the Whistler lakes are busy. One Mile is usually much quieter.

The beach park makes it easy to mix paddling with swimming.

One Mile Lake Canoe Rental FAQ

How far is One Mile Lake from Whistler?

About 25 minutes north, on the edge of Pemberton.

Is One Mile Lake good for beginners?

Yes. It's small, calm and has an easy launch.

Do you deliver to Pemberton?

Yes. One Mile Lake is one of our regular delivery spots. Delivery is priced by location.
