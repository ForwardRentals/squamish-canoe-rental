Paddling Nita Lake

Nita Lake, delivered.

Nita Lake sits just behind Whistler Creekside, small and sheltered, with less traffic than Alta or Lost Lake. On a calm evening the surface turns to glass and reflects the mountains.

It's not a big paddle. It's a beautiful one, and the one we suggest to couples and anyone who wants a quiet hour on the water.

Good to Know

Nita Lake Canoe Rental FAQ

Where is Nita Lake?

Just behind Whistler Creekside, at the south end of the valley.

Is it busy?

Usually quieter than Alta or Lost Lake, which is a big part of its appeal.

Is it good for beginners?

Yes. It's small and sheltered, and usually calm.
