Paddling Alice Lake

Alice Lake, delivered.

Alice Lake Provincial Park sits just north of downtown Squamish, with four small lakes, sandy beaches and one of the busiest campgrounds in the Sea to Sky. Alice Lake itself is calm, sheltered and warm by midsummer, which makes it about as easy as canoeing gets.

It's also our most popular multi-day delivery. Families book a canoe for the length of their camping stay, we drop it at the site, and the kids paddle every morning before the beach gets busy. Day visitors get the same service at the day-use area.

Tips for Alice Lake

Paddle early. Summer weekends get busy on the beaches by late morning, but the water stays roomy.

Book the full camping stay so the canoe is there whenever you want it. We pick up on check-out day.

Pair the paddle with the Four Lakes Trail loop for a full park day.

Good to Know

Alice Lake Canoe Rental FAQ

Can you deliver a canoe to my Alice Lake campsite?

Yes. Campsite delivery is our most popular Alice Lake booking. Give us your site number and dates, and we'll drop off and pick up at the site.

Is Alice Lake good for beginners?

It's one of the best beginner lakes in Squamish: small, sheltered and calm, with no current or tide to deal with.

Can I bring my dog in the canoe?

Yes. Dogs are welcome in our canoes. Check the park's current pet rules for beaches and trails.
