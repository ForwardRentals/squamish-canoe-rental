Renting a Board in Whistler

Skip the box-store inflatable. Rent a real board for the day.

Most people who want to paddle in Whistler end up grabbing a cheap inflatable from a big-box store, pumping it up in a parking lot, and wrestling it into the trunk. Where You Can Paddle

Whistler SUP Rental Spots

Alta Lake

The classic Whistler paddle. Warmest of the lakes, with three launch options — Rainbow Park, Lakeside Park and Wayside Park. Glass-calm on a still morning.

Lost Lake

Closest to the village and walkable from Whistler Upper. No motorboats, swim-safe, and quieter past the main beach. A great spot to rent your first board.

Green Lake

Glacial turquoise water with dramatic Wedge and Armchair views. Bigger and colder than Alta — rent here if you're a confident paddler and get out early for the colour.

Nita Lake

Tucked behind Creekside, small and almost always mirror-flat. Easy parking and less traffic than Alta or Lost. Prime sunset-paddle territory.

Alpha Lake

Family-friendly lake at the south end of Whistler with a beach park and dock. Calm, shallow shoreline — the easy pick with kids.

River of Golden Dreams

Rent a board, drop at Rainbow Park (Alta Lake) and finish at Meadow Park (Green Lake) for a one-way downstream paddle — one of the best half-days in BC. A real board, not an inflatable the river can pop.

Good to Know

Whistler SUP Rental FAQ

Where can I rent a paddleboard in Whistler?

With us — and we bring the board to the lake. We deliver to Alta Lake (Rainbow, Lakeside, Wayside), Lost Lake, Green Lake, Nita Lake, Alpha Lake, and put-ins for the River of Golden Dreams. No storefront, no line-up.

How much does a Whistler SUP rental cost?

We're day-rated with multi-day and group discounts. Call or text (604) 849-8898 for current rates. A full day on a real board beats a $20 box-store inflatable that the river puts a hole in.

Do you deliver, or do I pick the board up?

We deliver. Pick a lake and a time, we meet you at the launch with the boards and gear, and we pick up at the same spot after. No roof rack, no pump, no hauling.

Can I rent a SUP to paddle the River of Golden Dreams?

Yes. We drop at Rainbow Park (Alta Lake) and pick up at Meadow Park (Green Lake) for the one-way downstream run. Best in late June – July with good flow. Mention it when you book.

How far in advance should I book?

Same-day works if we have boards free, but in July and August we recommend a day or two ahead, especially on weekends.

What if I've never paddled a SUP before?

No problem — Lost Lake and Alpha Lake are calm, beginner-friendly water, and Jeremy will walk you through the basics at drop-off.
