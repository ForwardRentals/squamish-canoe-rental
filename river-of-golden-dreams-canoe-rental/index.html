Paddling River of Golden Dreams

River of Golden Dreams, delivered.

The River of Golden Dreams winds from Alta Lake through wetlands and forest to Green Lake. It's narrow and twisty, mostly gentle, and one of the most memorable paddles in BC. The full trip takes about 2-3 hours.

It's one-way, so the shuttle is what makes it easy. We drop the canoe at Rainbow Park on Alta Lake and pick it up at Meadow Park on Green Lake. No car shuffle, no paddling back upstream.

Local Knowledge

Tips for River of Golden Dreams

Late June through July usually has the best flow.

Expect tight bends, overhanging branches and the odd shallow spot. Steering matters more than speed.

Good to Know

River of Golden Dreams Canoe Rental FAQ

How long is the River of Golden Dreams paddle?

About 2-3 hours from Rainbow Park on Alta Lake to Meadow Park on Green Lake, depending on water levels and how often you stop.

Do you shuttle the canoe?

Yes. We drop at Rainbow Park and pick up at Meadow Park, so it's a one-way trip.

Is it suitable for beginners?

Confident beginners do it every summer, but you'll need to steer through tight bends. Book a calm lake first if you've never paddled.
