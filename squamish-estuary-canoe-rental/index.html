Paddling Squamish Estuary

Squamish Estuary, delivered.

The Squamish Estuary is where the river meets Howe Sound: a wide, flat maze of channels, marsh and mudflats, with the Chief and Mount Garibaldi on the skyline. It's one of the most photographed views in Squamish, and it's best seen from the water.

The tide decides everything here. Go near high tide and the channels are full and easy. Go at low tide and you might be dragging the canoe across mud. We help you time your launch so you get the good version.

Good to Know

Squamish Estuary Canoe Rental FAQ

Do I need to check the tides to paddle the Squamish Estuary?

Yes. The estuary is tidal, and low tide can leave channels too shallow to paddle. We'll help you time the launch when you book.

Is the estuary good for beginners?

In calm conditions near high tide, yes. Afternoon wind off Howe Sound can make it harder, so mornings are best.

What wildlife will we see?

Bald eagles, herons, waterfowl and seals are common. The estuary is an important wildlife area, so keep your distance.
