Paddling Green Lake

Green Lake, delivered.

Green Lake sits at the north end of the Whistler valley, and its glacial colour is the reason people come. Wedge Mountain and the Armchair tower above it, and on a still morning the reflections are unreal.

It's bigger and more open to the wind than Alta or Lost Lake, which is why we recommend canoes over paddleboards here and suggest some prior experience. It's also the finish line for the River of Golden Dreams.

Local Knowledge

Tips for Green Lake

Go early for calm water and the best colour.

The water is cold, so wear your PFD and stay near shore if the wind builds.

Meadow Park is the take-out for the River of Golden Dreams.

Good to Know

Green Lake Canoe Rental FAQ

Is Green Lake good for beginners?

We recommend some prior canoe experience. It's larger and more exposed to wind than Whistler's other lakes.

Why is Green Lake so green?

Glacial silt (rock flour) suspended in the water scatters light, which gives it that turquoise colour.

Canoe or paddleboard on Green Lake?

Canoe. It handles the wind and chop much better than a SUP when conditions change.
