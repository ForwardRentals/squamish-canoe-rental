Paddling Cat Lake

Cat Lake, delivered.

Cat Lake is a small forest lake at a recreation site just north of Brohm Lake. There are no motorboats, no current and no tide, just a quiet loop of water you can paddle around in under an hour.

That's exactly why we send first-timers here. It's the place to learn how a canoe turns and to give the kids a paddle. It's also a good warm-up before trying something bigger, like the Squamish River.

Local Knowledge

Tips for Cat Lake

Use it as a warm-up lake before the Echo Lake river crossing or a Howe Sound trip.

Weekday mornings are quietest. The recreation site gets busy on summer weekends.

It's small enough to circle in an hour, so add a picnic and a swim.

Good to Know

Cat Lake Canoe Rental FAQ

Is Cat Lake good for beginners?

It's the easiest canoe outing in Squamish: small, calm and motor-free, with no tide or current. It's ideal for first-timers and kids.

Do you deliver to Cat Lake?

Yes. We bring the canoe, paddles and PFDs to the launch and pick up afterward.

How long does it take to paddle around Cat Lake?

It's a small lake, so a relaxed lap takes under an hour. Most people stay longer to swim and explore.
