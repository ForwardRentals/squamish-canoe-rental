If you're staying in Squamish for a long weekend, you don't want to spend the morning figuring out tie-downs and the afternoon getting the canoe back off the roof in a campsite parking lot. Where We Deliver

Squamish Canoe Launch Spots

Alice Lake Provincial Park

Our top multi-day delivery. Four interconnected lakes, campground sites, and easy launches. Drop at your campsite — we'll pick up at the end of your stay.

Squamish River

A real river paddle for intermediate paddlers — we deliver upstream and arrange shuttle pickup downstream. Includes the famous cross-river paddle to the Echo Lake trailhead — see Echo Lake canoe rental.

Howe Sound (Britannia, Porteau, Furry Creek)

Saltwater paddling with seals, eagles, and Anvil Island views. Half-day, full-day, and overnight trips. Best in calm morning conditions.

Cat Lake

Small, calm, motor-free. The easiest canoe day in Squamish — perfect for first-timers, kids, and dogs.

Brohm Lake

Warm summer lake with a shoreline loop trail. Great combination of paddling and swimming on a hot day.

Browning Lake (Murrin Park)

Quiet provincial park lake with cliffs and good evening light. Compact paddle, big scenery.

Squamish Estuary

Tidal flatwater paddling with the Chief overhead. We help you time the tide for an easy launch.

Mamquam Blind Channel

Calm tidal channel behind downtown. A short, relaxed paddle steps from a coffee shop.

Good to Know

Squamish Canoe Delivery FAQ

Where do you deliver canoes in Squamish?

Alice Lake, Brohm Lake, Cat Lake, Browning Lake, the Squamish River, Mamquam Blind Channel, the Squamish Estuary, and Howe Sound launches like Britannia Beach and Porteau Cove. Campsites and Airbnbs too.

How many people fit in your canoes?

Up to 3 paddlers per canoe — 2 adults plus a kid and gear, or 3 adults paddling. Plenty of room for a dog and a cooler.

Can I rent for multiple days?

Yes — multi-day rates available. Alice Lake campsite drop-offs and Howe Sound overnight trips are popular.

Do I need canoe experience?

For calm lakes, no. For the Squamish River or open Howe Sound paddling, at least one prior canoe trip is recommended. Call (604) 849-8898 and Jeremy can sense-check your plan.

What about paddleboards?

We do those too — see paddleboard delivery in Squamish.
