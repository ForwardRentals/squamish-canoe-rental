Paddling Mamquam Blind Channel

Mamquam Blind Channel, delivered.

The Mamquam Blind Channel runs along the edge of downtown Squamish, a sheltered arm of water between the town and the estuary. It's the most convenient paddle in town, with no driving up the highway, and you're on the water in minutes.

It's a relaxed, short outing that works when you only have a couple of hours, and it connects to the wider estuary for anyone who wants to keep going.

Tips for Mamquam Blind Channel

Like the estuary, the channel is tidal. Higher tides make for easier paddling.

It's a good way to finish a downtown afternoon. Paddle first, then dinner.

Confident paddlers can continue out toward the estuary.

Good to Know

Mamquam Blind Channel Canoe Rental FAQ

Where is the Mamquam Blind Channel?

It runs along the edge of downtown Squamish, between town and the Squamish Estuary.

Is it tidal?

Yes. It's connected to Howe Sound, so the water level changes with the tide. We'll help you pick a good time.

How long is a typical paddle?

Most people spend one to two hours. It's a short, easy outing.
