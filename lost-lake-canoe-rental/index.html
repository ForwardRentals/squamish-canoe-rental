Paddling Lost Lake

Lost Lake, delivered.

Lost Lake is the closest lake to Whistler Village. It's tucked into the forest at the end of a short walk or ride, with a beach, a dock and trails all the way around. There are no motorboats, so the water stays calm and quiet.

It's a small lake, perfect for families with young kids in the canoe, a first paddle, or a quick outing between other Whistler plans.

Lost Lake Canoe Rental FAQ

Can I walk to Lost Lake from Whistler Village?

Yes. It's walking distance from the village and the Upper Village.

Is Lost Lake good for kids?

Very. It's small, calm and motor-free, with easy swim breaks.

Do you deliver to Lost Lake?

Yes. Tell us your time and we'll meet you with the canoe and gear.
