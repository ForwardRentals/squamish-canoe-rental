Paddling Alta Lake

Alta Lake, delivered.

Alta Lake is the main lake in the Whistler valley and the warmest of the bunch. Three parks line its shore: Rainbow Park on the west side, and Lakeside and Wayside on the east. Each has its own launch, so you can pick whichever suits your day.

It's also the start of the River of Golden Dreams. Rainbow Park is the drop-off for that one-way paddle down to Green Lake.

Local Knowledge

Tips for Alta Lake

Mornings are usually glass-calm, and afternoon thermals can pick up.

Rainbow Park has the best views across to the mountains.

Add the River of Golden Dreams for a one-way downstream finish.

Alta Lake Canoe Rental FAQ

Where on Alta Lake do you deliver?

Rainbow Park, Lakeside Park or Wayside Park. Pick the one that suits you.

Is Alta Lake good for beginners?

Yes, especially in the calm morning hours.

Can I paddle from Alta Lake to Green Lake?

Yes, by the River of Golden Dreams. See our River of Golden Dreams canoe shuttle.
